My story

I’m as comfortable in a tag manager as I am in a boardroom. That’s rare in marketing — and it’s exactly why AI came naturally.

I started in 2015 doing SEO and PPC for real-estate and local businesses in Pakistan. Within two years I was leading digital delivery at an international agency, where I ran 50+ growth projects for international clients across SaaS, fintech, IT and eCommerce.

From 2022 I moved into leadership roles with German tech companies — first as CMO and marketing consultant for a developer-tools SaaS, then leading app growth for a professional networking app, acquisition and lifecycle for a B2B analytics SaaS, and owning the marketing P&L for an AI / Web3 SaaS company, where we cut CAC by 22%.

Today I’m based in Dubai. I lead digital growth in UAE healthcare, and I’m building the marketing function of a real-estate group in Saudi Arabia from scratch — AI-first, with agents and automation doing the heavy lifting from day one. In between, I advise founders on how to put AI to work across their growth stack.
