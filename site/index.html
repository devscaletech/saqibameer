07 — FAQ

Good questions

Still unsure? Ask me directly — I reply within two working days.

Do you lead strategy, or do you also build?

Both — that is the point. I set the strategy, own the numbers and lead the people, and I can still open the ad account, the tag manager or the automation builder myself. Nothing gets lost between the deck and the dashboard.

What kind of companies do you work with?

Brands with product-market fit that sell online and want to grow more efficiently: DTC, eCommerce and SaaS teams are the best fit, plus healthcare and service businesses with a clear digital funnel.

Can you lead our team or agencies?

Yes. As a fractional or full-time growth lead I set priorities, run the experiment roadmap, manage agencies and freelancers, and report to founders and the board in plain numbers.

Do you work with clients outside the UAE?

Yes. I have worked remotely with teams in Germany and across Europe since 2022, alongside clients in the GCC, MENA and South Asia. Dubai time (GMT+4) overlaps well with both Europe and Asia.
